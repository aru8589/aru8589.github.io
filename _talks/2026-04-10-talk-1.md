<div style="text-align: justify; margin-left: 20px; margin-right: 20px;">
Machine learning (ML) is increasingly used in catalysis to connect catalyst composition, structure, reaction conditions, and catalytic performance. The central idea is to learn relationships from existing experimental or computational data and use them to predict promising catalysts or reaction conditions. The future of computational catalysis is not simply replacing chemical theory with machine learning, but combining chemical knowledge, physical models, data, and uncertainty-aware learning to make catalyst discovery more efficient and more scientifically informative.
</div>
