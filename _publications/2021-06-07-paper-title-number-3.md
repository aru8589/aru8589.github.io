excerpt: 'This work investigates an octahedral Fe₈₅ nanocluster as an electrocatalyst for the electrochemical nitrogen reduction reaction. Density functional theory calculations were used to examine associative and dissociative reaction pathways, catalytic activity, current density, hydrogen evolution competition, and Fe dissolution stability. The study demonstrates a low NRR overpotential for the Fe₈₅ nanocluster, together with reduced hydrogen evolution activity and enhanced stability compared with the periodic Fe(110) surface.'
