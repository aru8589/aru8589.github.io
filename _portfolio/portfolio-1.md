Iron phthalocyanine is a molecular catalyst candidate featuring a central iron atom coordinated by four nitrogen atoms within a conjugated macrocyclic ligand. This project investigates whether ligand coordination and spin-state rearrangement can alter the electronic structure. The central ambition is to connect molecular-scale quantum behaviour with catalytic function and develop a computational framework for evaluating potential catalyst-design strategies.
