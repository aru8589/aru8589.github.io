--- title: "Active-learning Accelerated Discovery of Stable Electrocatalysts for Green Hydrogen Production" excerpt: "Description
" collection: portfolio --- This proposal aims to develop a predictive framework for layered oxyhydroxide-based electrocatalysts using advanced first-principles calculations combined with active learning models. The project will focus on theoretical design and screening of 3d/4d/5d series of transition metal doped oxyhydroxide minerals, with stability assessed through electrochemical criteria. Mechanistic insights into oxygen evolution reaction (OER), oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) and hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) will be obtained via adsorption energies (∆Ead) and free energy changes(∆G). Reaction pathways will be systematically explored to evaluate reaction energetic and catalytic feasibility
